# HeartBits AI Video Model and Service Disclosure

**Effective Date: July 13, 2026**  
**Last Updated: July 13, 2026**

## 1. Overview

HeartBits (the "Platform") uses an artificial intelligence video generation model to provide AI video generation and related creative services. This disclosure explains the video model used by the Platform, its purpose, how generated content is processed, and the responsibilities associated with using the service.

## 2. Video Model Information

- **Model Name:** Gork 4.5
- **Model Type:** Artificial intelligence video generation model
- **Integration Source:** Accessed through a model service interface integrated with the Platform
- **Primary Purpose:** To generate videos from creation instructions, character settings, image materials, and other parameters submitted by users
- **Supported Use Cases:** AI character videos, animated character content, short video clips, and other video creation features supported by the Platform

## 3. Video Generation Process

When a user submits a video generation request, the Platform sends the text prompt, character settings, required creation parameters, and any relevant materials voluntarily provided by the user to the Gork 4.5 model service for video generation.

After a video has been generated and returned to the Platform, it enters a post-generation human content review process. The video may remain in a processing or pending-review status until the review is complete.

Only videos that comply with applicable laws and regulations, the HeartBits User Agreement, the Content Safety and Review Policy, and the Prohibited Content Policy may be displayed or delivered to users. Videos that do not pass review will be blocked and will not be displayed or delivered.

## 4. Content Safety Measures

The Platform maintains a prohibited-term and prompt-filtering library to identify high-risk generation requests and support content review. The library covers risk categories including illegal activity, violence and terrorism, sexual content and child safety, hate and harassment, fraud and impersonation, privacy violations, intellectual property infringement, dangerous goods, illegal drugs, gambling, cyber abuse, and attempts to circumvent review controls.

The Platform performs a routine update of the filtering library once per month and may issue emergency updates in response to changes in law, regulatory requirements, risk events, user reports, or review findings.

## 5. User Responsibilities

Users must ensure that all prompts, character settings, reference images, reference videos, and other submitted materials are obtained and used lawfully. Users must possess all necessary intellectual property, likeness, privacy, and other applicable rights or authorizations.

Users may not use Gork 4.5 or any HeartBits video generation feature to create, distribute, or store unlawful, infringing, fraudulent, sexually explicit, violent, hateful, harassing, deceptive, deepfake, or otherwise prohibited content. Users may not create synthetic videos of real individuals without authorization or attempt to evade Platform safeguards by using altered spellings, split words, homophones, encoded language, coded expressions, prompt injection, or similar methods.

## 6. Limitations of AI-Generated Videos

AI video generation is probabilistic and may produce visual defects, abnormal motion, inconsistent audio or lip movements, inaccuracies, bias, factual errors, or results that do not match a user's expectations. The Platform does not guarantee that generated videos will be accurate, authentic, unique, or suitable for any particular purpose.

Before publishing, commercially using, or otherwise distributing a generated video, users must independently review it and satisfy any AI-content disclosure, authorization, and compliance obligations applicable to the intended use. Users may not present generated videos as records of real events or use them to mislead others.

## 7. Review Records

To support content safety management, regulatory review, dispute resolution, and security audits, the Platform retains necessary video review records for **180 days**. The categories of retained information and the applicable handling practices are further described in the HeartBits Content Safety and Review Policy and Privacy Policy.

## 8. Data and Privacy

The Platform processes relevant information only to the extent necessary to provide video generation services, conduct content safety reviews, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations. Additional information about data processing, user rights, and security measures is available in the HeartBits Privacy Policy.

## 9. Service Changes

The Platform may update the model version, integration method, or scope of the video generation service due to model upgrades, service improvements, provider changes, or compliance requirements. This disclosure will be updated when a material change occurs.
