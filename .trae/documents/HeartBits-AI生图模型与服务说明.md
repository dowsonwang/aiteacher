# HeartBits AI Image Model and Service Disclosure

**Effective Date: July 13, 2026**  
**Last Updated: July 13, 2026**

## 1. Overview

HeartBits (the "Platform") uses an artificial intelligence image generation model to provide AI image generation and related creative services. This disclosure explains the image model used by the Platform, its purpose, how generated content is processed, and the responsibilities associated with using the service.

## 2. Image Model Information

- **Model Name:** Image 2
- **Model Type:** Artificial intelligence image generation model
- **Integration Source:** Accessed through a model service interface integrated with the Platform
- **Primary Purpose:** To generate images from text prompts, character settings, and other creation parameters submitted by users
- **Supported Use Cases:** AI character portraits, character images, scene images, and other image creation features supported by the Platform

## 3. Image Generation Process

When a user submits an image generation request, the Platform sends the text prompt, character settings, required creation parameters, and any relevant materials voluntarily provided by the user to the Image 2 model service for image generation.

After an image has been generated and returned to the Platform, it enters a post-generation human content review process. The image may remain in a processing or pending-review status until the review is complete.

Only images that comply with applicable laws and regulations, the HeartBits User Agreement, the Content Safety and Review Policy, and the Prohibited Content Policy may be displayed or delivered to users. Images that do not pass review will be blocked and will not be displayed or delivered.

## 4. Content Safety Measures

The Platform maintains a prohibited-term and prompt-filtering library to identify high-risk generation requests and support content review. The library covers risk categories including illegal activity, violence and terrorism, sexual content and child safety, hate and harassment, fraud and impersonation, privacy violations, intellectual property infringement, dangerous goods, illegal drugs, gambling, cyber abuse, and attempts to circumvent review controls.

The Platform performs a routine update of the filtering library once per month and may issue emergency updates in response to changes in law, regulatory requirements, risk events, user reports, or review findings.

## 5. User Responsibilities

Users must ensure that all prompts, character settings, reference images, and other submitted materials are obtained and used lawfully. Users must possess all necessary intellectual property, likeness, privacy, and other applicable rights or authorizations.

Users may not use Image 2 or any HeartBits image generation feature to create, distribute, or store unlawful, infringing, fraudulent, sexually explicit, violent, hateful, harassing, deceptive, deepfake, or otherwise prohibited content. Users may not attempt to evade Platform safeguards by using altered spellings, split words, homophones, encoded language, coded expressions, prompt injection, or similar methods.

## 6. Limitations of AI-Generated Images

AI image generation is probabilistic and may produce visual defects, inaccuracies, bias, factual errors, or results that do not match a user's expectations. The Platform does not guarantee that generated images will be accurate, authentic, unique, or suitable for any particular purpose.

Before publishing, commercially using, or otherwise distributing a generated image, users must independently review it and satisfy any disclosure, authorization, and compliance obligations applicable to the intended use.

## 7. Review Records

To support content safety management, regulatory review, dispute resolution, and security audits, the Platform retains necessary image review records for **180 days**. The categories of retained information and the applicable handling practices are further described in the HeartBits Content Safety and Review Policy and Privacy Policy.

## 8. Data and Privacy

The Platform processes relevant information only to the extent necessary to provide image generation services, conduct content safety reviews, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations. Additional information about data processing, user rights, and security measures is available in the HeartBits Privacy Policy.

## 9. Service Changes

The Platform may update the model version, integration method, or scope of the image generation service due to model upgrades, service improvements, provider changes, or compliance requirements. This disclosure will be updated when a material change occurs.
